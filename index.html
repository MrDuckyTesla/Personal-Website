Hello, I am Nico Lamas (aka MrDuckyTesla), welcome to my personal website!


Feel free to mess around and take a look at all the available pages. Reloading the page will give you a random background, and they all have mouse interactions, so have some fun playing around with it!

I made this website with the goal of being minimalistic in its javascript usage, while also being as dependency free as possible. This site uses just p5.js for the backgrounds and cloudflare to check for bots.

Both the websites frontend and backend are completely open source and available on my github.
